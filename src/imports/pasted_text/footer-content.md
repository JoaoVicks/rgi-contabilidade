Implement the Footer section at the very bottom of the RGI Contabilidade landing page.

Do NOT modify, recreate, or redesign any previous section.

Do NOT generate any images.

Use the existing RGI logo and existing project assets.

The Figma design is the source of truth.

The objective is to faithfully reproduce the footer's visual hierarchy, proportions, spacing, typography, colors, and positioning.

────────────────────────────────────

## 1. FOOTER CONCEPT

The footer should be minimal, elegant, professional, and informative.

It should act as the final information layer of the website.

Do not turn the footer into another marketing section.

Do not add unnecessary content.

The footer should feel spacious and calm.

────────────────────────────────────

## 2. BACKGROUND

Use the deep burgundy / dark wine color from the existing RGI design system.

The background should occupy the entire width of the footer.

Do not constrain the background to the normal content container.

The burgundy background must extend from the left edge to the right edge of the viewport.

────────────────────────────────────

## 3. FOOTER CONTENT CONTAINER

Place the footer content inside a centered content container.

The content container should:

• Have a reasonable maximum width.
• Maintain consistent horizontal padding.
• Align with the main content width used throughout the website.
• Prevent content from touching the viewport edges.

The background remains full-width while the content remains constrained.

────────────────────────────────────

## 4. MAIN FOOTER LAYOUT

On desktop, use three primary content areas:

LEFT:
RGI logo + company positioning statement.

CENTER:
Quick navigation links.

RIGHT:
Office and contact information.

The three areas should be horizontally aligned.

Use a clean grid or flex layout.

Do not create visible borders or vertical separators.

────────────────────────────────────

Below the logo, include the positioning statement shown in the design:

accounting solutions."

Maintain the proportions from the Figma design.

## 11. SPACING

Preserve the generous whitespace shown in the Figma design.

The footer should not feel crowded.

Maintain:

• Comfortable spacing between the three main content areas.
• Clear separation between the main content and developer credit.
• Generous vertical padding.
• Consistent horizontal alignment.

Do not compress all the content into a small area.

────────────────────────────────────

## 12. TYPOGRAPHY

Use the existing RGI typography system.

The visual hierarchy should be:

1. RGI logo
2. "Links rápidos"
3. Contact information
4. Navigation links / positioning statement
5. Developer credit

Avoid large typography.

The footer should remain visually quieter than the sections above it.

────────────────────────────────────

## 13. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Desktop:

Use the three-column composition shown in the Figma design.

Left:
Brand

Center:
Quick links

Right:
Contact information

Developer credit remains near the bottom-right.

Tablet:

Reduce the horizontal spacing while preserving the three content areas if possible.

If necessary, allow the content areas to adjust proportionally.

Mobile:

Stack the footer content vertically.

Recommended order:

1. RGI logo + positioning statement
2. Quick links
3. Contact information
4. Developer credit

Keep everything left-aligned or centered according to the existing mobile design system.

Do not allow horizontal overflow.

The developer credit should remain clearly separated from the main footer content.

────────────────────────────────────

## 14. ACCESSIBILITY

Ensure:

• All navigation links are keyboard accessible.
• Contact links are keyboard accessible.
• Visible focus states.
• Sufficient contrast between text and burgundy background.
• Icons do not replace meaningful text.
• Screen readers can understand the purpose of each link.

Do not rely exclusively on icons for navigation.

────────────────────────────────────

## 15. ANIMATION

Keep footer animation extremely subtle.

Do not animate the footer when the page loads.

Use only subtle hover and focus transitions for interactive elements.

Avoid:

• Parallax
• Large movement
• Bounce
• Scaling
• Decorative animations

The footer should feel stable.

────────────────────────────────────

## 16. DESIGN FIDELITY

Do not add:

• Newsletter forms
• Social media sections
• Additional navigation categories
• Testimonials
• Statistics
• Extra CTAs
• Maps
• Contact forms
• Additional company information

unless explicitly present in the Figma design.

The footer should remain minimal.
